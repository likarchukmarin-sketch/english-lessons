Consequences

What happens if…?

Finish each sentence yourself before revealing a possible answer.

…they smell bad.

…the kitchen smells.

…your home gets messy.

Важливо

Discover the Grammar

What do these sentences have in common?

If you don't take out the garbage, the kitchen smells.
If you leave wet clothes in the washing machine, they smell bad.
If you don't tidy up regularly, your home gets messy.
  • 1. Are we talking about one specific future situation?
  • 2. Or something that usually happens?
  • 3. Which tense do we use after IF?
  • 4. Which tense do we use in the result?

ZERO CONDITIONAL

IF + Present Simple → Present Simple

Use it for: facts, rules, things that usually happen, cause and effect.

If you don't clean your sponge, it gets dirty.

Мета

Guided discovery — студентка сама формулює здогад перед правилом.

Можливі помилки

Може сказати "will" в обох частинах — не виправляти одразу, дати дійти самій на наступному слайді.

Zero Conditional Check

Choose the ending

If you don't wash your sheets regularly…
Zero Conditional: both parts use Present Simple — no "will", no "would".
If you leave dishes in the sink…
A general result — Present Simple on both sides.
If you don't dry the dishes…
Fact / usual result → Zero Conditional.
If you don't take out the garbage…
Cause and effect, always true → Present Simple + Present Simple.
If you don't sweep the floor…
Same pattern — a usual, repeated result.
If you don't tidy up…
Zero Conditional — a rule about what always happens.

Мета

Закріпити форму Zero Conditional через розпізнавання, перш ніж переходити до First.

Якщо легко

Попросити додати власний приклад на кожен пункт усно.

Zero → First

Wait… is it always Zero Conditional?

If I don't do the laundry regularly, I have no clean clothes.
If I don't do the laundry tonight, I'll have no clean clothes tomorrow.

What is the difference?

ZERO CONDITIONAL

General / usual result

If I don't do the laundry regularly, I have no clean clothes.

FIRST CONDITIONAL

Real future possibility

If I don't do the laundry tonight, I'll have no clean clothes tomorrow.

IF + Present Simple → WILL + base verb
(never "will" after IF)

Мета

Контраст загального правила vs конкретної майбутньої ситуації.

Можлива помилка

"If I will do the laundry tonight..." — одразу м'яко виправити після цього слайду.

Detective

Zero or First?

1. If I don't clean regularly, my apartment gets messy.
ZERO — a usual, repeated result ("regularly").
2. If I don't do the laundry tonight, I'll have no clean clothes tomorrow.
FIRST — a specific, real future situation ("tonight"/"tomorrow").
3. If you leave wet clothes in the washing machine, they smell bad.
ZERO — always true, a fact.
4. If I have guests tomorrow, I'll tidy up the living room.
FIRST — a specific future plan.
5. If you don't clean your sponge, it gets dirty.
ZERO — a general fact.
6. If I finish work early, I'll do the grocery shopping.
FIRST — a real possibility today.
7. If you don't take out the garbage, the kitchen smells.
ZERO — a general rule.
8. If I don't have time tonight, I'll do the dishes tomorrow.
FIRST — a specific plan for tonight/tomorrow.

Мета

Розпізнавання Zero vs First за контекстом (regularly/usually vs tonight/tomorrow).

Follow-up

Попросити пояснити "чому" після 2-3 відповідей, не після кожної.

Housework Emergency 🚨

What will you do?

Use First Conditional at least twice.

Situation 1 — Your friends are coming in 2 hours. Your apartment is a mess.
"If my friends are coming in two hours, I'll tidy up first."
Situation 2 — You have no clean clothes and you need to leave tomorrow morning.
Situation 3 — Your washing machine suddenly stops working.
Situation 4 — You're exhausted after work, but the garbage is full and there are dirty dishes everywhere.
"If I don't have enough energy, I'll only clean the kitchen and leave the rest for tomorrow."

Мета

Вільна продукція First Conditional у реалістичних "кризових" ситуаціях.

Важливо

Не показувати готові answers заздалегідь — лише опційний приклад за кнопкою, якщо застрягла.

Real Life Decisions

What would you do first?

do the dishes do the laundry sweep the floor take out the garbage tidy up

You only have 30 minutes before leaving home.

Which three things will you do first? Why?

e.g. "If I only have 30 minutes, I'll take out the garbage first because…"

Мета

First Conditional + обґрунтування вибору (because) — природний, реалістичний контекст.

Bonus Level ⭐

What if…?

SECOND CONDITIONAL

IF + Past Simple → WOULD + base verb

Use it for: imaginary / unlikely situations.

If I had a cleaner, I would never do the ironing.

First: If I have more time tonight, I'll clean the kitchen. → real possibility

Second: If I had more free time, I would clean the whole apartment. → hypothetical

Мета

Короткий bonus — лише якщо залишається час. Не перевантажувати теорією.

Якщо мало часу

Можна пропустити слайди 12-13 повністю.
