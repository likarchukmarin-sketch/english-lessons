Who Are You? People In Your Life

Speakout B1, Unit 1A 50 min Speaking-first + diagnostic
Lesson goal: by the end of the lesson, you can describe 3–4 important people in your life (who they are, what they're like, what you do together), naturally choosing between Present Simple and Present Continuous, with minimal pauses to search for words.

1. Warm-up — quick retrieval

5 min

Answer right away, without thinking too long about each one. The goal is speed, not perfect accuracy.

Quick-fire questions (spoken, from your teacher)

  • How often do you talk to your family?
  • Who are you spending the most time with these days?
  • What do you usually do at weekends?
  • What are you working on right now (a project, a book, a habit)?
  • Who is your oldest friend, and how did you meet?

2. Vocabulary — people & character

8 min

This vocabulary is probably already familiar — the goal here isn't to learn something new, but to bring it back quickly.

Chunks for talking about relationships

get on well with look up to stay in touch with take after rely on catch up with
get on well with — to have a good relationship with someone · look up to — to admire and respect someone · stay in touch with — to keep contacting someone · take after — to be similar to a parent or relative · rely on — to depend on someone · catch up with — to meet and talk after a while apart

Personality adjectives — check how fast you can recall these

reliable easy-going generous sociable hard-working patient stubborn supportive
reliable — you can trust them to do what they say · easy-going — relaxed, doesn't get stressed easily · generous — happy to give time/money/help · sociable — enjoys being with people · hard-working — puts in a lot of effort · patient — doesn't get annoyed while waiting · stubborn — doesn't change their mind easily · supportive — helps and encourages you

Try it out loud

Pick 3 adjectives and 2 chunks, and say 3 sentences out loud about real people in your life — no writing.

3. Grammar — Present Simple vs Continuous

10 min

This is grammar you already know. The tasks below are diagnostic — let's see how automatic your choice of tense is, and whether there's a trap with state verbs.

Notice the difference

1. My sister lives in Lviv, but she's staying with us this month.
lives — a permanent fact (Present Simple); is staying — a temporary situation right now (Present Continuous).
2. I usually see my grandparents on Sundays, but this week I'm seeing them on Friday instead.
usually see — a habit/routine; am seeing — a specific temporary plan for this week.
3. She's working on a big project at the moment, so she's quite busy these days.
is working — a temporary period, happening now/around now; is (busy) — Present Simple, a current description.

Spot the mistake (state verbs)

✗ I'm loving spending time with my family.
✓ I love spending time with my family.
love is a state verb — it isn't used in the continuous.
✗ He's having three brothers.
✓ He has three brothers.
have (meaning "to possess") describes a state, not an action.
✗ She's understanding the situation really well.
✓ She understands the situation really well.

Adverbs of frequency — order them mentally from 0% to 100%

never rarely / hardly ever sometimes often usually always

Remember: these words go before the main verb, but after "to be" — I usually call her. / She's usually busy.

4. Guided practice

5 min

7. Self-check

1 min
  • I can describe important people in my life using personality traits and chunks.
  • I can correctly choose between Present Simple and Present Continuous in spontaneous speech.
  • I don't use the continuous with state verbs (love, have, understand...).
  • I can answer follow-up questions without long pauses.

8. Homework

2 min

Voice message, 30 seconds

Introduce yourself as if to a new acquaintance: who you are, who you spend time with, what you're doing right now. Use at least 3 personality adjectives from today's lesson, and choose correctly between Present Simple and Present Continuous.

Goal: active retrieval of vocabulary with no visual support — exactly what matters most for you right now.
