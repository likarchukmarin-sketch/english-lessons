Stage A · Enter Forks

The new girl in the rain

Forget the English lesson for a minute. You are in Forks, Washington.

5 min
Forks, Washington. It rains almost every day. Grey sky, green forest, a very small school. A new girl arrives from a sunny city. Everyone looks at her. But she is looking at five students who sit alone and never seem to belong.
Three questions · one at a time

1 · Notice

Close your eyes for a moment. What can you see, hear and feel in Forks?

  • What colour is the town?
  • Do you feel calm, tense or lonely? Why?

2 · Wonder

Why is a rainy, quiet, forest town a good place for a story about secrets?

  • What could people hide here?
  • Would you like to live there? Why or why not?

Challenge: How is a small town different from a big city for someone with a secret?

3 · Predict

The new girl notices something strange about the five students. What do you think she will discover?

  • What makes you think that?
  • Is it dangerous for her? Why?

Stage B · Read the clues

Three exhibits from Forks High School

Open one exhibit. Describe what you can see. Then give two explanations: an ordinary one and a secret one.

12 min
Exhibit A
Lunchtime. Five students sit together at one table. Their trays are full: pizza, fruit, a drink. Nobody takes a bite. They only talk quietly and look at the door.

What is strange about this table?

  • What can we actually see?
  • Give one ordinary explanation and one secret explanation.
  • Which is more convincing? Why?
Ordinary explanation
Maybe they … / They could be …
Secret explanation
I have a feeling that … / It might be …

Challenge: What would you need to see to be sure?

Look at the trays, not the people. Why would someone carry food they do not eat?
Story fact In the story, the Cullens are vampires. They carry food so they look like ordinary students.
Exhibit B
Biology class. The only free seat is next to Edward. As Bella sits down, he leans away, covers his face and looks angry. The next day his seat is empty. He is absent for days.

Why does Edward react like this to a girl he has never met?

  • What are you sure about? What are you only guessing?
  • Use the ladder: is this must, might or can't?
  • Why does he disappear instead of staying and explaining?
Theory 1
He must … / He might …
Theory 2
Another possibility is … / It can't be …

Challenge: If you were Bella, what would you think after the first day? What would you do?

Think about the opposite of dislike. Could a strong reaction mean something else?
Story fact · spoiler Edward is fighting a very strong instinct around Bella. Staying away is his way to protect her.
Exhibit C
Forks has one rule: it rains. But on the rare sunny day, five desks stay empty. Nobody in school seems to notice. Bella does.

What do the Cullens do when the sun comes out? What might this mean?

  • Past deduction: what must have happened to them yesterday?
  • Why might this be an easy clue to miss?
  • Which is stronger evidence: Exhibit A, B or C? Why?

Detective language

The confidence ladder

ALMOST SURE
must be / must have + past participle
He must be hiding something.She must have noticed the clue.
POSSIBLE
might / could be / might have + past participle
She might know the truth.They might have planned it.
IMPOSSIBLE (for me)
can't be / can't have + past participle
He can't be serious.It can't have been an accident.

Now = modal + base verb (must be). Before = modal + have + past participle (must have seen). Here must means “I am almost sure”, not “it is an obligation”.

Say it · then check it

Use the ladder

Say your own sentences aloud:

  1. Edward didn't eat his lunch. He must …
  2. Yesterday his seat was empty. He might have …
  3. Bella can't …, because …
  4. The Cullens aren't at school today. They could …

Teacher: ask “How sure are you? Why?” after each sentence.

Quick check: choose the best phrase.

    Homework

    The Easter Egg Hunter

    Find one or two details in the Twilight films. Record a short audio message in English.

    10–20 min

    Your mission

    One really good detail is better than two weak ones. You can also use a case from our lesson that we did not discuss.

    Fact check rule: if you only read it on a fan page, say “fans say” or “some people think”. Do not say “it is a fact”.

    Your audio plan

    1. The detail: Which film? What did you notice?
    2. The theory: What does it mean?
    3. The evidence: Where did you find it? Is it confirmed or a fan theory?
    4. Your opinion: Do you believe it? Why or why not?

    Language help

    I found a hidden detail in … / It might be a reference to … / Fans say that … / I'm not completely sure, but … / In my opinion it is / isn't a coincidence because … / I read this on …

    Starting point: Twilight Saga Wiki: Easter eggs (a fan wiki: check claims before calling them confirmed).
