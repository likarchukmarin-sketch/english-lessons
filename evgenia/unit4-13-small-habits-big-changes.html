Explanation, 5 minutes

Your challenge is your choice. Keep it small, safe and realistic. If something gets in the way, it’s fine to change the plan; the goal is to notice what happens, not to be perfect.

Part B, choose one

Interview someone you know (a friend, a family member, a colleague) about a habit they changed. Ask them in any language, but tell the story to your teacher in English in the next lesson.

  1. What habit did you build or break?
  2. Why did you decide to change it?
  3. Was it hard? Did you ever give up and start again?
  4. What helped you stick to it?
  5. What advice would you give to someone who wants to change a habit?

Write a letter of 8–10 sentences to a friend who says: “I spend hours on my phone every evening. I want to stop, but I always give up after two days.”

  • Show that you understand the problem.
  • Explain why habits are hard to break (use ideas from the text).
  • Give 2–3 practical tips.
  • Finish with some encouragement.
