The Transformation: What's Changing?

Bella is now a vampire. She is discovering her enhanced senses, extraordinary strength and speed, and learning to control her thirst. She is adapting to a completely different way of life.

A new body. A new set of rules.

Everything feels unfamiliar.

These are ongoing processes. Bella is still learning, testing, and adapting — she has not necessarily made each new skill part of her effortless routine yet.

Grammar Reference

Time expressions are clues, not automatic answers. Meaning and context determine the choice.

Present Simple vs Present Continuous
Present Simple

Usual, stable, generally true

  • Habits and routines: Bella often reads in the evening.
  • General facts and stable situations: Bella lives in Forks.
  • States, opinions and preferences: Bella thinks Edward is mysterious.
  • Possession: Bella has a red truck.
Present Continuous

Now, temporary, still changing

  • Actions happening now: Bella is talking to Edward.
  • Temporary situations in the current period: Bella is spending more time with the Cullens these days.
  • Developing changes and ongoing processes: Bella is learning to control her thirst.
  • Repeated behaviour with always to express irritation: He is always interrupting her!

Some verbs change meaning

Opinion: thinkBella thinks Edward is fascinating. (*think* = have an opinion)
Mental activity: thinkBella is thinking about her future. (*think* = consider)
Possession: haveBella has a red truck. (*have* = possess)
Activity: haveBella is having dinner with the Cullens. (*have* = take part in an activity)

Stative verbs such as know, believe, love, need and own are not normally used in continuous forms when they describe states. Some verbs can be continuous when their meaning changes.
