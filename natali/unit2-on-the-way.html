Excuse Me, How Do I Get There?

Ask for directions, understand the answer and give directions yourself. A lesson for walking around a new city, for example in Belgium.

50 minutes audio + city map Could you tell me…? Turn left · Go straight
🔊 Audio settings — choose the best voices on this computer
Default voices: Google US English (woman) and Google UK English Male (man), in Chrome. If they are not there, the lesson uses Microsoft Mark or the best voice it can find. The voices read the phrases, the map directions and the homework dialogue.

6

Speaking: The City Map

13 min

One map. Three games. No roleplay: just ask, answer and listen.

Three games:
  1. You ask: Natali is at the start. She asks the teacher the way to the place. The teacher answers.
  2. You explain: The teacher asks the way. Natali explains the way on the map.
  3. Listen and click: Natali listens to directions (▶) and clicks the place on the map.
🚶 In every game you can walk on the map: use "Go straight" (one block), "Turn left" and "Turn right" under the card. Then press "I'm here!" to check.
Ready?
Choose a game and press "Next card".
