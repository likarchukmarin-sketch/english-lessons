At the Doctor's

Say what hurts, answer the doctor's questions and give advice. A lesson for the doctor, the pharmacy and helping a friend who feels bad.

50 minutes body map I have a… · My … hurts You should…
🔊 Audio settings — choose the best voices on this computer
Default voices: Google US English (woman) and Google UK English Male (man), in Chrome. If they are not there, the lesson uses Microsoft Mark or the best voice it can find. The voices read the body words, the phrases and the doctor in the simulation.

A Friend Feels Bad

6 min

Your friend is ill. How can you help? Choose the best idea and say it.

You should…It is a good idea.
"You should drink water. You should rest."
You shouldn't…It is not a good idea.
"You shouldn't work. You shouldn't eat fast food."
The formulaФормула: порада
You should / You shouldn't + verb
Question: "Should I see a doctor?" — "Yes, you should."
What is the best advice?Яка найкраща порада?
Should or shouldn't?Should чи shouldn't?
Advice cards: say the adviceКартки порад: скажи пораду вголос
How it works:
  1. The card shows a friend's problem. Read it or listen (▶).
  2. Say two pieces of advice: one with "should", one with "shouldn't".
  3. Click "Teacher: model" to check.

How it works:

  3. The doctor answers. Play again with another problem.
