Plans or Decisions?

going to and will for quick decisions. Level A1+. 50 minutes.

By the end of the lesson I can:
  • talk about my plans for Saturday or next week with going to, and ask questions;
  • make a quick decision with will when something changes;
  • choose between the two forms in short everyday situations.

2. Remember: going to

Retrieval, 7 min

First think, then check.

What do we use for plans?

Спочатку скажи сама. Потім натисни кнопку.

I am going to cook dinner.
She is going to call Kate.
They are not going to watch a series. subject + am / is / are + going to + verb. The yellow word never disappears.

Question: What are you going to do? Are you going to go out?

3. Surprise!

Discovery of will, 8 min

You have a plan. Then something changes. What will you do? Answer in speaking first.

What did you notice?

will = a decision at the moment
I'll / we'll + base verb no -s, no "to". I'll call. We'll meet. Not: I'll calls. Not: I'll to call.

will = рішення в момент мовлення.

Chunks to recognise: What will you do?   What if it rains?   If it rains, I'll stay at home.   I won't go out. (won't = will not)

Discovery questions appear after you open the frame on 3 cards.

5. Emma's Saturday

Listening, 7 min

Emma is the same character as in the last lesson. Listen twice.

Speed

Listening 1. What is it about?

Listening 2. What did Emma plan? What did she decide?

Заплановано (going to) чи вирішила зараз (will)?

Hi! This is Emma. Tomorrow is Saturday, and I have a plan. In the morning, I'm going to do the laundry. Then I'm going to meet my friend Kate in the park. We're going to have a coffee and take some photos. In the evening, I'm going to cook dinner. Oh, wait. I'm looking at the weather app. It says: rain tomorrow. OK, no problem. I'll call Kate. We'll meet in a cafe, not in the park. And after dinner, I'll watch a series at home.
