Could You Do Me a Favor?

Everyday problems and how to ask for help. After this lesson you can ask someone to help you, ask permission, and say yes or no politely.

50 minutes video lesson Could you…? Can I…? Sure! / Sorry, I can't.

Your 8 Phrases

7 min

Eight phrases. Only eight. They work at a station, in a café, in a hotel — everywhere.

Could you / Can you…?You want someone to do something for you.
"Could you help me, please?"
Can I / Could I…?You want to do or take something.
"Can I borrow your charger?"
How to work with these cards:
  1. Read the situation in Ukrainian. Say the English phrase out loud.
  2. Click "Check" to see the answer. Click ▶ to hear it. Say it again.
  3. Click the star on the 4 phrases you want to remember most.

Your Turn: Ask and Answer

17 min

Two games. In one you answer a request. In the other you ask for help. No roleplay, just talk.

How it works:
  1. You answer: the teacher reads the request. Natali answers "yes" or "no + reason", as the card says.
  2. You ask: Natali reads the situation and asks the teacher for help. The teacher says "yes" or "no".
  3. Mixed: the cards change by themselves.
